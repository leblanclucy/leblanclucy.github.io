title: DFT, or Applying Quantum Chemistry to Molecular Properties
---
<html>
  Instead of solving the Schrodinger equation for every electron in a system, density functional theory (DFT) looks at electron density (or the kinetic energy density of electrons) to calculate numerous properties and accomplish many tasks, including geometry optimization, generating conformers, solvation energy, reaction energy barriers, and much more.
