--- layout: default --- Data Science by LeBlanc Tutorials in data visualization as well as computational techniques like molecular dynamics and density functional theory using software like OpenMM and ORCA.

Resources

For an applied approach to density functional theory, I recommend the 2022 review by Bursch et al. (doi: https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202205735) This gives you just enough background on the quantum chemistry itself before diving into exactly what functionals, basis sets, and implicit solvents to use and when for whatever application you're interested in (conformers, reaction barriers, complexes involving metal ions, and so on).

For further reading on ADMET and machine learning, the original paper by Swanson et al. (2024) (doi: https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae416) showing how ADMET-AI (a platform that predicts druglike properties using a graph neural network) was developed is quite helpful but brief. Another paper detailing an ADMET initiative fusing both wet lab data and machine learning is one by Fraser et al. (2026) (doi: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73410-8) and goes much deeper into how ADMET prediction works and what it's based on.
